§ 02Refusals

What a run refuses to claim

This is not a disclaimer bolted to the bottom of a page. These are the limitations of the run itself, printed as data every time — in the terminal, in the HTML report, and in the machine-readable JSON. Below is what a default run says, in its own words.

  1. 01 Critical

    Conformal bounds were calibrated on SYNTHETIC data. Every interval, and therefore every PASS, is exercisable rather than earned. Fit a real screen with cas13guide train before treating a bound as a guarantee.

  2. 02 Critical

    Activity came from the built-in heuristic, which is a shaped guess and not a trained model. It saturates: a perfect duplex scores about 0.93 regardless of sequence, so the ranking among well-matched guides carries little information. Fit a model on a real screen, or plug a published one into the ActivityModel seam.

  3. 03 Critical

    Collateral (trans) cleavage is not predicted for this assay. Once activated, Cas13 degrades bystander RNA, and that scales with target abundance rather than with off-target homology — so no specificity score on this page bounds it. Assay it directly.

  4. 04 Critical

    Off-targets were searched over circrna, coding, mito, ncrna, pre_mrna, repeat, rrna, synthetic, viral in the sequences supplied to this run. Anything not loaded is invisible: a guide is specific against what you gave it.

The four marks above are not a fixed banner: they are what this run could not establish. A tool that only prints its confidence leaves you to guess at the rest.

§ 04Decision

How it decides

Three steps, in this order. The order is the design: what happens first cannot be undone by what happens later.

No weighting can rescue a guide the gate failed. A refusal is not a low score — it is a refusal.

Which end of the interval gets read

Metrics are read at the bound that is unfavourable to the guide. Coverage at its lower bound, hazard at its upper. A high point estimate with a wide interval is not evidence of efficacy.

What that procedure cannot do

Thirteen review rounds have fixed 78 defects, one regression test class each, named for the defect it guards. The method finds disagreements between two descriptions of one run. It cannot find a formula that is coherent, documented, tested against its own output, and scientifically wrong.

Whether the activity model predicts real knockdown is a question for a wet-lab screen, and nothing in this repository stands in for one.
